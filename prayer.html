Matthew 6:9-13 · Geneva Bible

After this manner therefore pray ye —

10Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done even in earth, as it is in heaven.

11Give us this day our daily bread.

12And forgive us our debts, as we also forgive our debtors.

13And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil: for thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory for ever. Amen.

Before you fast

Fasting in Scripture was never only about food — Daniel kept a partial fast, and Isaiah calls justice and generosity the fast God chooses. If you are pregnant, diabetic, on medication, have any health condition, or have ever struggled with eating, please speak to a doctor before going without food. Nothing here is instruction on how long to fast.
