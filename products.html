Custom messages

Sometimes the words matter more than the word. Tell me who it's for and what you're trying to say, and I'll write it.

This is a different thing from a wallpaper. There, the word is the subject and any note is a small addition. Here, the writing is the piece — a birthday, a leaving, a wedding, an apology, a thank-you that's been owed for years. Delivered as a written piece you can read, print or frame. Where a WordSpin word fits the occasion, I'll build it around one; where it doesn't, I won't force it.

How this works

  1. 1 You send a brief. The button opens a short set of questions. Answer what you can — a vague brief is still a brief, and I'll ask about the rest.
  2. 2 I reply with a price and a date. Nothing is priced in advance, because nothing here is the same twice. You'll know both before anything is made.
  3. 3 You see it before it's finished. A draft comes to you first. Three rounds of changes are included; beyond that we'll talk.
  4. 4 It arrives. Files by email. Anything printed or framed is posted, and I'll tell you the shipping cost up front.
